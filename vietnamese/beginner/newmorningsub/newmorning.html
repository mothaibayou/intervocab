A New Morning

🎧 Listen to the Story

Every morning, Sam wakes up at 7:00. The sun is bright, and the room is warm. He drinks a big glass of cold water and eats a piece of toast. He likes toast with honey.

Sam works in a small library in the city. He walks to work because the weather is nice. He likes his job because he loves books. At the library, he helps people find stories. He is a happy person.
