Jumbotron Disaster

“Be Sure Your Sins will Find You out”

🎧 Listen to the Story

On a warm summer evening in 2025, sixty-five thousand voices were singing along at a concert of the rock group Coldplay.

Lost in the ocean of fans, two people stood close, arms wrapped tightly around each other.

To the stadium camera operator looking for lighthearted crowd footage to feature on the arena's massive Jumbotron, they were the perfect shot for the "Kiss Cam."

The lovey dovey couple suddenly saw themselves starring in their own video on the giant Jumbotron. Their romantic embrace broke instantly, replaced by sheer panic.

The woman instantly shielded her face and spun away. The man ducked, physically dropping to the floor to hide from the lens.

From the stage, frontman Chris Martin watched the bizarre reaction play out and joked to the entire stadium, "Either they're having an affair or they're just very shy."

It turned out to be the former. Within hours, social media sleuths identified the mortified couple, instantly setting the internet on fire in a global frenzy of salacious fascination and gossip.

The secretive lovers weren’t just any pair; they were the top executives of Astronomer, a prominent tech firm. The man was CEO Andy Byron. The woman hiding her face was Kristin Cabot, the company’s head of Human Resources.

The modern workplace has few boundary lines as rigidly enforced as the one between a CEO and HR, but the betrayal cut far deeper than corporate optics. Both executives were married to other people and both had children.

The fallout was swift, total, and played out in the public eye. Within days, Astronomer launched a formal investigation, forcing both of them to leave the company. Simultaneously, their personal lives shattered.

Cabot, who was married to Andrew Cabot—the CEO of Privateer Rum— filed for divorce in court less than a month after the video went viral. Meanwhile, Byron’s wife, Megan, immediately scrubbed his last name from her social media accounts and moved out of the family home.

A year later, the wreckage continues. Cabot’s children were so traumatized they needed therapy. Cabot recently opened up about the "disaster of epic proportions," detailing her ongoing unemployment, the loss of her hard-earned career identity, and the fact that she and Byron no longer speak. It is perhaps the ultimate modern cautionary tale of the digital age.

A famous expression from the Bible says, “Be sure your sin will find you out.” A single second on a Jumbotron that instantly went around the world taught this lesson to a pair of adulterous ‘love birds’ the hard way with devastating impact not only on them but on their families as well.

And remember: “The internet lives forever!”
