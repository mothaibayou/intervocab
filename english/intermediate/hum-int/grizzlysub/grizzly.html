Man Who Loved Bears

Story Series

🎧 Listen to the Story

Timothy Treadwell loved grizzly bears more than anything else. For 13 summers, he traveled to National Park in Alaska. He wanted to live with the bears, protect them, and show the world that they were not just dangerous monsters. He called himself the "Grizzly Man."

Timothy lived in a small tent right in the middle of bear territory. He did not carry a gun or bear spray because he believed in peace. Over the years, he got very close to the animals. He gave them names like Cupid, Tabitha, and Mr. Chocolate. He talked to the bears, sang to them, and sometimes even kissed them. He took beautiful videos of his life with these giant creatures and became famous. He truly believed the bears were his friends and that they understood his love.

However, wildlife experts and rangers were very worried. They warned Timothy many times, saying: "Grizzly bears are beautiful, but they are wild predators. They do not have human feelings, and they are not your friends." Timothy did not listen. He felt he was part of their world and that his connection with nature would keep him safe.

In October 2003, Timothy and his girlfriend, Amie, stayed in Katmai later than usual. October is a dangerous time in Alaska. The friendly bears Timothy knew had already gone to sleep for the winter. The bears that were still awake were old, hungry, and desperate for food before the deep snow arrived.

One rainy night, a large, hungry bear attacked their tent. Sadly, both Timothy and Amie were killed and eaten by the bear. It was a terrible tragedy that shocked the world.

Timothy's story is beautiful, sad and controversial. Many admired him for believing that "bears need love too!". Yet others thought he had bear scat for brains! However, his story leaves us with an important lesson: we can love and admire wild bears but we must always respect their power and keep a safe distance. If they are hungry they will see you not as the extraordinary person that you are, but simply as a tasty dinner!
