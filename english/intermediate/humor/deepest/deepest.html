Dr. Hương Trần wanted to land an important position at a prestigious hospital in his city. When he was hired for the job, he and his family moved to a beautiful new house. The next day some beautiful flowers were delivered to them, with a note that said, “Deepest Sympathy”. Sorry for your loss.

Naturally, Dr. Trần was stunned and quite upset to receive this note just when he was celebrating his promotion to a high status job. He angrily drove straight to the flower shop that had sent the flowers to him. He complained bitterly about this outrage.

The proprietor of the shop was shocked. He apologized profusely. He was not aware that he had made a mistake. Then he realized how bad his error really was and he became nauseous and turned green. Dr. Trần was alarmed and said, “Hey don’t take it THAT hard!”. The shop owner replied, “What we did to you was not the worst of it. In our confusion we sent the note meant for you to the family of a dead guy, delivered to the funeral parlour. The note said, “Congratulations on your new position!”
