Six passengers were traveling in a compartment on a train. Most were polite and well-behaved. But one of them was a rude young punk with long, oily hair who kept annoying the others with smoking and playing awful music from a boom box.

Finally, to everyone’s relief, the young ruffian got off the train at a train stop. He had two heavy suitcases. No one volunteered to help him take the bags off the overhead shelf. He struggled out of the compartment and off the train with the two bags.

When he had lugged his heavy bags about 30 meters from the train, one of the passengers shouted out the window to him, “Hey, you left something!”. The young dirtbag turned around and returned to the train, hauling his heavy bags with him. He arrived at the train huffing and puffing, quite out of breath. He gasped, “What did I leave behind?”

Just then the train began moving forward again. The passenger shouted to the lazy lout, “A very bad impression!” As the train pulled away, he heard all the passengers laughing their heads off! The young punk, still gasping for air, was fit to be tied, but at the same time he realized he had received his just deserts.
