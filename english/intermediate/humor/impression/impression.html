John was exploring America in his new car. One evening he was looking for a small hotel called Sun Motel. He saw a man sitting on the side of the road. The man appeared to be in an inebriated state. But John was in a pickle – it was late, he needed information and he decided to take a chance and ask the drunken fellow for directions.

He stopped his car and said, “Hello sir, can you tell me where the Sun Motel is?” John was pleasantly surprised when the man replied, “Absolutely. Let me show you!” The man got into John’s car, pointed his finger straight ahead and said, “Just go straight. I’ll tell you when to stop.” So John happily drove forward and waited for the man to tell him when to stop. They covered quite a bit of ground. Finally, the man said, “Ok, stop here!” John was surprised because there was no motel there, only a small house. John said, “Hey, this is not a motel. You promised to show me the way to the Sun Motel. What’s going on?”.

The man chuckled and said, “Of course it’s not a motel. It’s my house. Now I’ll tell you how to get to the Sun Motel. Turn around and go back five miles, it will be on your left.”
