A man was walking on a sidewalk when suddenly a voice said, “Stop! or a brick will fall on your head and you will suffer terrible consequences!”. He stopped, and at that moment a brick fell right in front of him.

The man was stunned but after a few moments, he continued on his way. A few minutes later, while he was crossing the street, the strange voice came again. “Stop! A car is about to hit you and you will meet disaster!” The man stopped instantly. At that moment a speeding car, driven by a drunken driver passed very close to him, nearly hitting him.

Now the man was frustrated and he called into the air, “Who are you? Where are you?”. “I am your guardian angel,” the voice answered, “I’m always there to save you from impending disasters”.

The man thought for a moment, then became very angry. He shouted at the guardian angel, “Where were you the day I got married?!”
