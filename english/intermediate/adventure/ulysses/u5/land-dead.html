Episode 5: The Land of the Dead

Odyssey Series

🎧 Listen to Episode 5

Following Circe’s parting instructions, the ships sailed to the absolute edge of the living world, where perpetual mists and dark clouds blocked out the sun forever. Ulysses stepped onto the bleak, frozen shore of the Underworld, tasked with a terrifying mission: he had to consult the blind prophet Tiresias to discover a way back to Ithaca.

To summon the spirits of the deceased, Ulysses dug a trench and poured solemn libations of milk, honey, and wine into the earth. The moment the offerings pooled, a swarm of pale, weeping ghosts surged from the shadows. Their Their eerie, breathless cries filled the air with a chilling dread that made his blood run cold.

Among the shadowy figures, Ulysses was heartbroken to recognize his own mother, who had died of grief waiting for his return. Though Though desperate to embrace her, he had to hold her spirit back with his drawn sword until the prophet arrived. When Tiresias finally stepped forward, he drank from the offering and spoke in a hollow, echoing voice.

The prophet delivered a grim warning. He foretold the relentless wrath of Poseidon and the deadly trials still to come—the the seductive call of the Sirens, the the snapping jaws of Scylla, and the sacred cattle of the Sun. Furthermore, he revealed that a chaotic mess awaited Ulysses at home, where arrogant suitors were devouring his wealth and pursuing his wife.

Ulysses marched back to his ship with a heavy heart, leaving the realm of shadows behind. He realized that the path home would demand more than just physical strength or clever tricks. To see his homeland again, he would have to conquer his own despair and wage a brutal battle against fate itself.
