Episode 9: The Bow Contest

The time had come. Penelope announced that she would marry any man who could perform a feat only Ulysses had ever accomplished: stringing his massive, heavy bow and shooting an arrow through the eyes of twelve axe-heads lined in a row.

One by one, the suitors tried. They were strong, arrogant men, but they lacked the technique.

They couldn't even bend the wood. The palace filled with laughter as they failed, their faces turning red with shame. Then, the ragged, old beggar stepped forward.

A murmur of mockery rippled through the hall, but Ulysses ignored it. He walked to the bow, testing its weight with a familiarity that made the suitors freeze.

He wiped the dust from the wood, pulled the string with fluid, terrifying grace, and let the arrow fly.

It whistled through the air, piercing every single axe-head with a clean, metallic thrum.

In the sudden, suffocating silence that followed, the beggar stood taller.

He dropped his cloak, and the light from the torches seemed to focus entirely on him.

He wasn't a beggar anymore; he was a storm waiting to break.

The suitors looked at his face and finally understood. Their dread was absolute. The king had returned, and there was nowhere left to run.
