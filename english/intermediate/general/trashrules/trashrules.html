On the door of the garbage room there is a sign with the image of a trash bin. Please sort the trash before throwing it away.

Trash that may be put into the garbage chute includes:

Household waste must be put into a trash bag and the mouth of the bag tightly tied before putting it into the chute.

Feces and waste from pets must be put into a trash bag, with the mouth tightly tied before putting it into the chute.

The trash bag must fit the size of the chute to ensure the bag falls down easily, without getting stuck or clogging the chute.

Throw trash as shown in the following picture:

Trash to put into the plastic basket includes: bottles and jars, carton boxes, books and newspapers, foam boxes

Trash in the form of string or long strips

DO NOT put the following types of trash into the garbage room:

Embers or chemicals, or adhesive substances.

Construction waste, waste of large size.
