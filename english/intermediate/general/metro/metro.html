Passengers must purchase a ticket and scan their ticket when boarding the train, in accordance with company regulations.

While on the train, passengers must never lean against the train doors. Do not touch the train doors. Passengers should pay attention to the safe distance between the train and the platform.

Maintain general order; maintain cleanliness; and take care of the property and equipment of the station and train.

Weapons, flammable or explosive substances, and any goods prohibited by law are strictly prohibited from being brought into the station or onto the train.

Do not cause disturbances, conduct sales, or engage in disrespectful behavior toward the train operator, company employees, or other passengers.

Do not enter restricted areas or obstruct employees while they are carrying out their duties.

Smoking, including electronic cigarettes, is prohibited in all forms within the station and on the train.

Do not bring bulky luggage. Animals are prohibited, except for small pets that can be carried in a closed bag. Do not bring food or drinks with strong odors onto the train.

Do not bring personal means of transportation, including ordinary bicycles, folding bicycles, balance bikes, scooters, or skateboards.

Do not organize artistic performances, shoot advertising videos, or take videos or photographs for commercial purposes in the station or on the train without prior permission.

Balloons are strictly prohibited in the station and on the train platforms.

The company will refuse service to children under 6 years old who are not accompanied by a guardian; intoxicated persons; mentally impaired persons; persons with infectious diseases; and persons with medical conditions for which a doctor has advised them not to travel.

Passengers must comply with the rules and regulations; follow the instructions and directions given by staff; and comply with disease-prevention regulations at stations and on trains.
