Many people have to work under the supervision of others. In most cases, employees do not get to choose their boss unless that supervisor is elected. In the following paragraphs, I will list the important qualities of an ideal boss.

First of all, a boss must be impartial. I believe it is very important to make a technical decision, or think about promoting someone, in an unbiased manner, and so on. For example, my friend is the boss of a dairy processing plant. It is a family business, so there are many relatives working there. However, he never promotes anyone just because they are his relative. I think that is an important quality of a boss.

Secondly, in my opinion, an ideal boss must be straightforward, patient, and a good listener. He should pay attention to the feelings of others, encourage them by raising their salaries, and listen to their suggestions for improving working conditions and labor productivity. For example, if he is not satisfied with the results, he should understand the root of the problem and show others how to fix it. Additionally, he needs to know how to resolve conflicts that arise among employees.

Another important quality of a boss is the ability to make accurate decisions and learn from the mistakes of others.

Finally, in my view, a good boss must know how to properly appreciate their employees because profits directly depend on them. In conclusion, I think a good boss should know how to make their employees love their work and encourage their enthusiasm.
