Story 5: Cooking Dinner

🎧 Listen to Story 5

Tonight, Tom wants to cook a special dinner for his family. He goes to the kitchen and puts on his apron. He wants to make pasta with tomato sauce. First, he cuts some onions and garlic. The smell is very good.

Then, he boils water in a large pot for the pasta. He adds the pasta and waits for ten minutes. While he waits, he cleans the table. His family comes home and they are very hungry. They all sit down to eat. The dinner is delicious, and everyone is happy.
