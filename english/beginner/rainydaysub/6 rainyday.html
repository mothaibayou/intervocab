Story 6: A Rainy Day

🎧 Listen to Story 6

Today, the weather is bad. It is raining hard, and the sky is grey. Elena stays inside her house. She does not want to go out in the rain. She sits on her comfortable sofa with a warm blanket.

She has a long book to read. She reads for two hours. Later, she makes a cup of hot tea and looks out the window. She watches the rain hit the glass. It is quiet and peaceful. She likes rainy days because they are perfect for relaxing at home.
