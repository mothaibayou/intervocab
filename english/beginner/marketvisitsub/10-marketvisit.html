Story 10: A Visit to the Market

English Learning Series

🎧 Listen to Story 10

Every Sunday morning, Amina goes to the local farmer's market. It is a busy place with many colorful stalls.

She likes to buy fresh fruit and vegetables directly from the farmers. Today, she buys a basket of strawberries, a loaf of warm bread, and some yellow flowers.

She enjoys talking to the people at the market. Everyone is friendly, and there is music playing nearby.

After she finishes her shopping, she sits on a bench to eat a sweet strawberry.

She loves the atmosphere of the market because it makes her feel connected to her community.
