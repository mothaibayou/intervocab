Story 7: Planning a Trip

🎧 Listen to Story 7

Next month, Elena is going on a trip to the mountains. She is very excited. She needs to pack her suitcase, so she makes a list. She writes down her warm clothes, her hiking boots, and her camera. She wants to take many photos of the beautiful scenery.

She also checks the weather report on her phone. The weather looks cold, so she remembers to pack a hat and gloves. She talks to her friend, who lives in the mountains, to ask about the best places to visit. Everything is ready for her adventure.
