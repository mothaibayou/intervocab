Story 4: A Fun Weekend

🎧 Listen to Story 4

It is Sunday afternoon. The sky is blue, and the wind is cool. Leo and his sister, Mia, go to the park. They have a soccer ball. Leo kicks the ball, and Mia runs fast to catch it. They laugh a lot because they have fun together.

After the game, they sit on a green bench to rest. They are thirsty, so they drink cold juice. They see many birds in the trees. "Look at that big bird," says Mia. Leo smiles. They stay in the park until the sun goes down.
