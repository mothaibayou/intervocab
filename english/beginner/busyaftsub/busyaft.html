Busy Afternoon

🎧 Listen

Today is Saturday, and Maria is very busy. She has many things to do. First, she goes to the grocery store because she needs food for dinner. She buys red apples, green vegetables, and some chicken.

After the store, she meets her friend, David, at a café. They drink coffee and talk about a movie. Maria wants to see it, but the tickets are expensive. So, they decide to watch a movie at home later. She is tired, but she is also very happy to see her friend.
