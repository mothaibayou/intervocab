Story 3: An Unexpected Discovery

🎧 Listen

Last week, Sarah decided to clean her old apartment. She moved a heavy chair in the corner of the living room. Suddenly, she saw a small, dusty box on the floor. She did not remember this box.

She opened it carefully. Inside, she found many old photographs from her childhood. There was a picture of her first dog, Buster, and a picture of her grandmother. Sarah felt very emotional. She spent the rest of the afternoon sitting on the floor and looking at the memories. It was a special day.
