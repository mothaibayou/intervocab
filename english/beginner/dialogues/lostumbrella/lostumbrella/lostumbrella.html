Hello, Elena. This is Mr. Miller. Are you staying warm inside? The weather is very bad today.

Yes, Sir. It is raining hard and the sky is grey. I am sitting on my comfortable sofa with a blanket and a cup of hot tea. It is very peaceful.

It is not peaceful for me! I took the bus to work today. When I got off the bus, I realized I did not have my black umbrella.

Oh no! Did you lose it? That was a gift from your daughter, right?

Yes, I felt very sad. But I just called the bus company. A kind driver has it! I am so relieved.

That is wonderful news! You need that umbrella.

Yes. Are you ready for your trip to the mountains next month?

Almost! I am making a packing list today. I need warm clothes, my hiking boots, and my camera.

Don't forget a hat and gloves, Elena. The weather report says it is very cold there.

Thank you. I will write that down on my list right now.
