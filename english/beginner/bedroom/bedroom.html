My Bedroom

🎧 Listen to the Story

This is my bedroom. Here is the bed. It has a pretty bedspread and a warm blanket. I cover up with it at night.

There is a soft pillow in a colorful pillow case on the bed. I pat the pillow to fluff it up.

Over there in the corner is my wardrobe. I hang my clothes in the wardrobe and put my shoes in the bottom of it. I keep its doors shut to keep moths from getting into and destroying the clothes.

This is the night stand next to the bed. There is a small lamp and a phone charger on it. I put my phone on the charger at night to charge it.

There is a bookshelf. I keep many books on the bookshelf.

I try to keep my room neat and clean. There is a desk with a chair. I push the chair under the desk when I sit down. I pull the chair out when I stand up. Right now, the desk is messy.

There is also a dresser in my room. The dresser has 4 drawers. I keep towels, wash cloths, mirrors, combs, hair brushed and other useful things in the dresser.

There is a big window in my room. I can see outside into the yard.

There is a painting hanging on the wall
